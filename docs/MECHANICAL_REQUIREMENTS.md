# Mechanical measurement checklist

Do not begin enclosure CAD from public outer dimensions alone. Capture each value
with a photo, datum definition, instrument, and uncertainty.

- T8L shell exterior, seam, grips, trigger/switch travel, lanyard point, screw/boss
  positions, wall thickness samples, and hand clearance.
- Main PCB outline, thickness, component maxima both sides, holes, USB, battery
  contacts, antennas and keepouts, cable exits, and service access.
- Both gimbal envelopes, fasteners, stick axes/travel, plugged connectors, cable
  sweep, trim/control modules, and switch threaded/soldered mounting.
- Battery cell bays, contact compression, polarity keys, door/latch, and insulation.
- Candidate display active/outer/glass/PCB dimensions, FFC exit/bend, mounting,
  optical stack, bezel, glare treatment, and landscape scan orientation.
- VRX, decoder, compute, Controller-I/O, antennas/connectors, heatsink/air volume,
  buttons, speaker/buzzer, USB-C, balance port and dust caps.
- Assembled center of mass, finger reach, neck-strap load, screen sight line, antenna
  clearance, drop impact paths, and service/disassembly sequence.
